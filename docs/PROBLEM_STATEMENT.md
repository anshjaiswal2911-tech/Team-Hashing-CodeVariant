## 🥀 The Core Problem: The Graveyard of Good Intentions

Over 90% of hackathon prototypes, student coursework, and indie developer side-projects are abandoned within weeks of inception. These projects rot silently inside private GitHub repositories, taking valuable authentication logic, API wrappers, database schemas, and creative ideas down with them.

### Three Critical Developer Pain Points:

1. **Lost Context:** When returning to an old repo months later, developers forget why it stalled, what was finished, and what bugs remained.
2. **Unclear Direction:** Builders lack objective decision-making frameworks to evaluate whether an unfinished project should be continued, pivoted, salvaged, or permanently archived.
3. **Restart Friction:** Starting an old project feels daunting, leading developers to rewrite the exact same boilerplate from scratch in every new hackathon.

---

## 🎯 Target Users

* **Students:** Coursework, class projects, and learning experiments that stalled due to final exams.
* **Hackathon Builders:** High-potential prototypes abandoned after hackathon submission deadlines.
* **Indie Developers:** Side-projects with strong technical foundations but uncertain market distribution.
